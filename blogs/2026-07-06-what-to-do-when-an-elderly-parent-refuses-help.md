When an elderly parent refuses help, the most effective approach is to understand what’s driving the resistance before pushing for a solution. As a family member, watching this resistance can be painful, especially when you know support is important for their safety and well-being. This guide explores compassionate, practical ways to encourage help without undermining dignity.
