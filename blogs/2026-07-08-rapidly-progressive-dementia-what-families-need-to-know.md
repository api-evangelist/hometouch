Rapidly progressive dementia (RPD) is a rare form of dementia that advances within weeks or months rather than years, causing a fast decline in memory, behaviour, and physical ability. Most forms of dementia develop over years. RPD moves faster, and this speed often leaves families struggling to understand what’s happening and what to do next.
